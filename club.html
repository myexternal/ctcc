The club

Teamworkbeyond borders.

What we're building in Tampere, a word from our chairperson, and the people who run the club.

Our vision

To make Tampere a home of hardball cricket in Finland, with a club that wins on the field and welcomes anyone who wants to play.

Our mission

We give cricket lovers in Tampere a place to play the real game: regular practice, league cricket with Cricket Finland, and a team that looks after its own. We bring the spirit of Sri Lankan cricket to Finland and share it with every player, wherever they come from.

  1. Play to win, the right wayCompete hard in every league match and respect our opponents, the umpires and the game.
  2. Teach the gameMost of us first played hardball in 2026. Anyone who joins gets coached from the basics up.
  3. Grow cricket in TampereMore players, more matches and better facilities for cricket in the Pirkanmaa region.
  4. One team, many backgroundsSri Lankan roots, open to everyone. What counts is how you play for the team.

A message from our chairperson

SJ

Sharada Jayaweera

Chairperson

A year ago this club didn't exist. Today we're Division 5 champions.

When we started Ceylon Tampere Cricket Club in 2026, most of our players had never played with a hard leather ball. Nine wins from ten matches later, we lifted the Division 5 trophy in Kerava, and Thushan De Zoysa was named the division's best batter.

None of that happened by luck. It happened because players came to practice after long working days, because supporters came out to watch, and because a handful of volunteers did the quiet work of organising fixtures, kit and grounds. Thank you, every one of you.

In 2027 we play in Division 4. The cricket will be harder, and that's exactly why we want to grow. If you've played cricket before, come and test yourself. If you never have, come anyway: that's how most of us started. And if you'd like to back a club that's going places, we'd love to talk.

Practice starts in January. I hope to see you there.

Sharada JayaweeraChairperson, Ceylon Tampere Cricket Club
